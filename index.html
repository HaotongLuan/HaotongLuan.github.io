Hi!

Haotong Luan

Undergraduate researcher in robotics

I am Haotong Luan, a third-year undergraduate student at the Future Technology School, Shenzhen Technology University, and a P-SRT student at Shenzhen X-Institute.

I work under the supervision of Prof. Wenzeng Zhang and am currently collaborating with Researcher Yuan Gao (Shanghai AI Laboratory) on visual-servoing control algorithms and end-effector design for robotic-arm grasping.

My work focuses on environment-adaptive robotic hands and mechanism design: linear-motion linkages, parallelogram mechanisms, underactuated and tendon-driven grippers, card-scooping hands, grasp-and-lift mechanisms, and idle-stroke transmissions.

I am also currently learning about VLMs, VLAs, and WAMs, and exploring VLM-based visual servoing for robotic arms.

栾皓童

机器人方向本科研究者

我是栾皓童，深圳技术大学未来技术学院大三本科生，深圳 X-Institute P-SRT 学员。

师从 张文增 副教授，从事欠驱动机械手研究；目前与上海人工智能实验室 高远 研究员合作，研究机械臂抓取中的视觉伺服控制算法与末端执行器设计。

研究方向：环境自适应机械手与机构设计，具体包括直线运动连杆、平行四边形机构、欠驱动与腱驱动夹爪、扒卡式手、抓取-提升机构、空行程传动等。

目前还在学习 VLM、VLA、WAM 三类模型，并探索基于 VLM 的机械臂视觉伺服控制。
